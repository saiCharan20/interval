Pushpa Teaser Breakdown: Colour, Long Nails and Conviction

Holi colours, a broken teal-and-orange rule, and a long little-finger nail: what the Pushpa teaser says before anyone speaks.

By Sai Charan, 9 Apr 2021, 2 min read

The teaser starts with colour. A blast of Holi colours, and the whole experience that follows is colourful in the same way. None of it is accidental. Everything here is planned and intentional.

Breaking the teal-and-orange rule

Look at the colour wheel and you will notice teal and orange sit in opposite directions. Put opposites together and things get more interesting, which is why teal and orange became a rule, and why it was running all over Instagram. Now push the saturation hard: teal turns blue, and orange turns reddish. The teaser’s whole colour concept is built by breaking the rule that way, and the posters released so far follow the same blue and red. I think that palette is drawn from how we paint our gods.

In graphic design there is a saying: to break the rules, you first need to know them. When you enter this profession, you will be handed brand guides and strange briefs, and you will meet new challenges every day. Knowing the rule is what lets you break it on purpose.

What the DI does

Digital intermediate, or DI, is the process of taking a film into a digital format and adjusting colour and visuals, much like editing photos, but far more detailed. Skin tones, the sky, every palette is decided deliberately. Colour correction happens here: brightness, contrast, the overall look. It is time-consuming and needs patience, and DI specialists work scene by scene to land the look. Creative teams discuss these details alongside the technical side of the shoot.

The shot

In the teaser’s key shot, it is not just Allu Arjun acting. It is the character, Pushpa. The shot probably went through several retakes, because the camera movement has to match exactly, tracking the same position from feet to hands. The coordination between cameraman and actor is crucial, and high-speed moves are hard to execute accurately.

The nail

The recent poster shows only Pushpa’s hand with a long fingernail, and this matters. I read that in Andhra Pradesh there was a belief that wealthy people kept a long nail on the little finger. If you do physical labour, lifting weights and working hard, you cannot keep long nails. If people work for you, you can. A long nail meant prosperity.

It takes courage to put a hidden meaning like that on a poster.

We rarely see that discussion in Hindi cinema. When you make something generic that most people understand, those conversations never happen. Here they took a stand and spoke to their own audience. It is a risk, and conviction is what carries it.

Allu Arjun looks very normal while doing absurd things, and Pushpa’s whole stardom is built on that. Just my opinion. So tell me: how did you like the teaser, and how did you like this breakdown?
